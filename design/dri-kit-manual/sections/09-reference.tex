\chapter{Reference}
\label{ch:reference}

This chapter provides complete field reference tables for all \texttt{gert.ops} types,
role semantics, environment variables, and error codes. Use this chapter as a quick-lookup
companion when authoring runbooks.

\section{Environment Variables}
\label{sec:ref-env-vars}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The following environment variables are recognized by \texttt{gert.ops} and affect runtime
behavior:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{lp{8cm}}
\textbf{Variable} & \textbf{Description} \\
\hline
\texttt{GERT\_OPS\_ROLE} & Declares the current user's role for this run. Overrides
  \texttt{meta.roles.*} assignment. Useful in CI environments. \\
\texttt{GERT\_OPS\_CHANGE\_ID} & Injects a change ticket ID without prompting. Used in
  automated pre-production pipelines. \\
\texttt{GERT\_OPS\_APPROVER\_TIMEOUT} & Overrides \texttt{meta.sla.approval-timeout} for
  this run. Duration string. \\
\texttt{GERT\_OPS\_EVIDENCE\_DIR} & Override the evidence output directory.
  Default: \texttt{.runbook/runs/<run-id>/attachments/}. \\
\texttt{GERT\_OPS\_FREEZE\_CHECK\_DISABLE} & Set to \texttt{1} to disable freeze window
  checks. Requires \texttt{risk: emergency} or superuser authority. \\
\texttt{GERT\_OPS\_BUNDLE\_ON\_COMPLETE} & Set to \texttt{1} to automatically write the
  evidence bundle JSON on run completion. \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\section{Error Codes}
\label{sec:ref-error-codes}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The following error codes are specific to \texttt{gert.ops}. Core gert error codes are
documented in the \emph{gert v2 Design Document}.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{llp{7.5cm}}
\textbf{Code} & \textbf{Exit} & \textbf{Description} \\
\hline
\texttt{OPS001} & 1 & Kit declaration missing: \texttt{kit: ops/v1} required for \texttt{ops.*} step types \\
\texttt{OPS002} & 1 & \texttt{ops.change-request} present but no \texttt{meta.roles.change-manager} declared \\
\texttt{OPS003} & 1 & \texttt{ops.incident} present but no \texttt{meta.roles.responder} declared \\
\texttt{OPS004} & 1 & \texttt{ops.manual} step has no \texttt{requires\_role} declaration \\
\texttt{OPS010} & 2 & Role check failed: executing user does not hold required role \\
\texttt{OPS011} & 2 & Approval gate rejected by approver \\
\texttt{OPS012} & 2 & Approval gate timed out with \texttt{on\_timeout: abort} \\
\texttt{OPS013} & 2 & Change-manager pre-execution approval rejected \\
\texttt{OPS014} & 2 & Freeze window active: change risk level \texttt{low}/\texttt{medium} blocked \\
\texttt{OPS020} & 3 & Required evidence not submitted before step completion \\
\texttt{OPS021} & 3 & Evidence submission by user without required role \\
\texttt{OPS030} & 4 & Rollback runbook not found at declared path \\
\texttt{OPS031} & 4 & Rollback runbook failed during automatic rollback injection \\
\texttt{OPS040} & 5 & Incident SLA resolution timeout: run suspended, IC acknowledgement required \\
\texttt{OPS041} & 5 & Incident commander takeover failed: declared commander not in \texttt{meta.roles} \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
