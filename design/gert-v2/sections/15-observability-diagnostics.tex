\chapter{Observability and Diagnostics}

Operational visibility is essential for production systems. This section covers structured logging format, metric emission points, distributed tracing integration (OpenTelemetry), and the structured diagnostics model for extension and tool interactions. It makes actionable the hardening claims from \S~7 by specifying concrete signals operators can use to debug failures, detect anomalies, and audit compliance.
